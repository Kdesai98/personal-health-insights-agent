# Personal Objectives

## Objective Stack

Krunal wants a personal health system that optimizes day-to-day life from a health perspective.

The main objective is not one metric. It is a balanced policy that improves body composition, performance, sleep, energy, focus, digestion, mood, and mental stability without creating unsafe tradeoffs.

## Primary Goals

- Gain more muscle.
- Cut some body fat.
- Improve sleep even if current sleep is already good.
- Maintain good mood.
- Improve digestion.
- Build very strong focus.
- Maintain strong energy.
- Improve athletic performance.
- Support healthy testosterone levels.
- Preserve peace and stability of mind.

## Current Known Inputs

- Oura is the only current wearable.
- Diet is vegan-vegetarian.
- Diet quality is believed to be balanced.
- Exercise is frequent: six to seven days per week depending on recovery.
- Training includes weight lifting and rowing.
- Rowing is not captured well by Oura.
- Meditation is strong and happens morning and night.
- No caffeine.
- Hydration is generally good.
- Daily vegan multivitamin.
- Ashwagandha at night.
- Saffron in the morning.

## Recommendation Permissions

The system may recommend:

- Workout structure.
- Training intensity.
- Recovery days.
- Meals.
- Protein targets.
- Bedtime.
- Sleep routine adjustments.
- Lab tests to discuss with a clinician.
- Low-risk behavior experiments.

The system must not recommend:

- Unverified supplements.
- Medication changes.
- Extreme cutting.
- Training through injury red flags.
- Medical diagnosis.

## Reward Priorities

Positive reward signals:

- Higher sleep quality.
- Stable or improved HRV relative to baseline.
- Appropriate resting heart rate relative to baseline.
- Better subjective energy.
- Better focus.
- Better mood.
- Better digestion.
- Workout completion.
- Progressive overload or improved performance.
- Adequate protein intake.
- Consistent meditation.
- Sustainable body composition trend.

Penalty signals:

- Poor sleep after a recommendation.
- Elevated resting heart rate with low recovery.
- Excess soreness or injury signs.
- Poor digestion after meals.
- Low energy or brain fog.
- Missed workouts due to bad planning.
- Overly aggressive calorie restriction.
- Recommendations that are too annoying to follow.
- Recommendations that contradict declared diet or lifestyle.
